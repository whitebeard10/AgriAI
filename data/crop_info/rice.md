# Rice Cultivation Guide

**Ideal Conditions:**
*   **Temperature:** 20-35°C (68-95°F)
*   **Rainfall:** 100-200 cm annually
*   **Soil:** Clayey or loamy soil, rich in organic matter, with good water retention capacity. pH between 5.0 and 7.0.
*   **Water:** Requires significant water, often grown in flooded fields.

**Key Practices:**
*   **Sowing:** Transplanting seedlings is common.
*   **Fertilization:** High nitrogen requirement.
*   **Pest & Disease Management:** Susceptible to blast, bacterial blight, and stem borer.

**Yield:**
*   Average yield: 3-6 tons/hectare, but can reach up to 10 tons/hectare with intensive farming.

**Market Value:**
*   Staple food globally, high demand. Prices vary based on variety and quality.
