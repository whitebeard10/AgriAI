# Wheat Cultivation Guide

**Ideal Conditions:**
*   **Temperature:** 15-25°C (59-77°F) during growing season, cooler temperatures for germination.
*   **Rainfall:** 30-90 cm annually, well-distributed.
*   **Soil:** Well-drained loamy soil, rich in organic matter. pH between 6.0 and 7.5.
*   **Water:** Moderate water requirement, sensitive to waterlogging.

**Key Practices:**
*   **Sowing:** Typically sown in autumn or early winter (winter wheat) or spring (spring wheat).
*   **Fertilization:** Requires balanced NPK, especially nitrogen.
*   **Pest & Disease Management:** Susceptible to rusts, powdery mildew, and aphids.

**Yield:**
*   Average yield: 2-5 tons/hectare, can be higher with improved varieties and practices.

**Market Value:**
*   Major global cereal crop, high demand for bread, pasta, etc. Prices influenced by global supply and demand.
