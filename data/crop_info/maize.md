# Maize (Corn) Cultivation Guide

**Ideal Conditions:**
*   **Temperature:** 20-30°C (68-86°F) during growing season.
*   **Rainfall:** 60-110 cm annually, well-distributed.
*   **Soil:** Deep, fertile, well-drained loamy soils. pH between 6.0 and 7.5.
*   **Water:** Requires adequate moisture, especially during flowering and grain filling.

**Key Practices:**
*   **Sowing:** Planted after the last frost, when soil temperatures are warm.
*   **Fertilization:** High nutrient requirements, especially nitrogen and phosphorus.
*   **Pest & Disease Management:** Susceptible to corn borer, armyworms, and various fungal diseases.

**Yield:**
*   Average yield: 5-10 tons/hectare, with potential for higher yields under optimal conditions.

**Market Value:**
*   Widely used for food, animal feed, and industrial products. Prices fluctuate based on global production and demand.
