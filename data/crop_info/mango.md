# Mango Cultivation Guide

**Ideal Conditions:**
*   **Temperature:** 24-30°C (75-86°F) for optimal growth and fruit development.
*   **Rainfall:** 75-250 cm annually, with a dry period before flowering.
*   **Soil:** Deep, well-drained loamy soils, slightly acidic to neutral (pH 5.5-7.5).
*   **Water:** Requires irrigation during dry spells, especially during fruit development.

**Key Practices:**
*   **Planting:** Grafted plants are preferred for early fruiting and true-to-type varieties.
*   **Fertilization:** Balanced NPK application, with higher potassium during fruiting.
*   **Pest & Disease Management:** Susceptible to powdery mildew, anthracnose, and fruit fly.

**Yield:**
*   Varies greatly with variety, age of tree, and management, typically 5-10 tons/hectare for mature orchards.

**Market Value:**
*   Highly popular tropical fruit, strong demand globally. Prices depend on variety, quality, and season.
